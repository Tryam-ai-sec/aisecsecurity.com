The problem

Most Indian businesses are online. Few have a security team.

Small and mid-sized companies, start-ups and agencies run websites, online stores, APIs and, more and more, AI chat and assistant features. Automated attacks such as SQL injection, cross-site scripting, credential stuffing and bot floods hit every public site, whatever its size.

Enterprise security tools assume a dedicated team to configure and watch them. Indian rules also expect more from businesses: CERT-In requires certain cyber incidents to be reported within 6 hours.

AISec exists to give these businesses protection they can switch on in minutes and understand without being security specialists.

Our product

Tryam: a managed web application firewall

A business points its domain at Tryam. Our edge filters every request and passes only clean traffic to the business's own server, with HTTPS included.

⛨

Stops common web attacks

Blocks SQL injection, cross-site scripting, path traversal, command injection and more, at the edge, before they reach the site.

◎

Detect mode first

New domains start in detect mode, so a business sees exactly what would be blocked before switching to block mode.

⇅

Rate limits and bot control

Per-site and per-path limits, for example on a login page. An address that floods a site is cut off automatically.

✦

APIs and AI applications

OpenAPI schema validation, JWT checks and object-enumeration alerts for APIs, and an AI firewall against prompt injection, jailbreaks and data leaks.

⎙

CERT-In report drafts

For an incident, the console prepares a draft CERT-In report from what the firewall saw, to help meet India's 6-hour reporting rule.

⌂

White label for partners

Agencies and managed service providers can offer Tryam under their own brand, with a partner console on their own domain.

Onboarding in three steps

  1. Add the domainand tell Tryam where the website's server lives.
  2. Prove ownershipby publishing one DNS TXT record.
  3. Point the A recordat Tryam. Protection and HTTPS start straight away.
